The Jaipur Royal Heritage

Sawariya Royal Luxury Jewellery

Founded in the Pink City of Jaipur by Om Mishra, Sawariya Royal Luxury Jewellery was born from a timeless reverence for the royal jewelers of Rajasthan. For generations, Jaipur has stood as the gemstone and jewelry capital of India, renowned for majestic Kundan, Polki, and imperial custom craftsmanship.

Our vision is to bring that same regal grandeur to every modern girl and lady across Jaipur and India. Every bracelet, earring, necklace, and ring is custom designer jewelry crafted with uncompromising attention to detail, using skin-safe, anti-tarnish protective coatings and radiant crystal detailing.
